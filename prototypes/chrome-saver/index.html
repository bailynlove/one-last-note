Designing notes that survive their sources

By Mina Chen · Published October 18, 2026

A useful note keeps its source, its structure, and the reason it mattered. Saving a page should feel like placing a document into a trusted collection, not starting an import job.

A selected passage needs the same clean structure and provenance as a full page, without silently becoming a full-page capture.

Capture is a reviewable snapshot

The content you preview is the content you save. Links, headings, lists, and images retain their meaning while active page code is left behind.
